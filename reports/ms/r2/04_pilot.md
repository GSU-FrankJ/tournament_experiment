## 9. Anomalies and what was not expected

1. **|peak| rose under s = 4 for the bin-balanced sampler at q = 60** (+0.0114 [+0.0034, +0.0185], 9 of 10 seeds), against the direction the smoothing floor predicts, and not under s = 16 at the same q (+0.0072 [-0.0038, +0.0174]). The window table (4.6) has the opposite order (s = 4: +0.0050 [-0.0002, +0.0108], s = 16: +0.0125 [+0.0058, +0.0201]).
2. **sigma_2(0) was above the 1/sqrt(s) prediction in every run** (section 6), by 1.3-10.2 %; the "+1" of the Beta variance accounts for at most 0.24 % of it (section 6); the rest is unexplained.
3. **The tail mean rose slightly with s** (`tail mean / e_2*(0)` -0.0002 to +0.0007; intervals exclude 0 in four of the eight cells: `NL_bb_s16` both q, `NL_st_s4` and `NL_st_s16` at q = 60). The change is at most 0.07 % of `e_2*(0)`.
4. **PPO diagnostics move with s** (section 5.1): KL and clip fraction per update rise with s, advantage SD falls. The experiment did not vary the LR at a given s, so it cannot distinguish a larger per-update policy movement at high s as the cause of the remainder from other explanations.
5. **One stratum value stands out**: `NL_st_s4` at q = 50, d < 0, middle stratum, mean signed error -1.152, the largest in magnitude of the 20 values in that column (the next is +1.106, `MS_s35a5` at q = 50; block `strata_mid`); not examined further.
6. **Check-to-check variation** of the seed-mean remainder at constant LR is up to 1.9 effort units at the six listed updates (2.08 over all consecutive constant-LR checks) (section 5.2), larger than the effects measured at the freeze; the pre-registered freeze comparison is a single checkpoint per run.
7. **No run was lost, re-run or flagged by a check**; all gates pass in all 120 runs, so criterion part (b) cannot fail in this round.
