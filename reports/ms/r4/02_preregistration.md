## 1. The question and the design

The T = 2 status pack is closed (`reports/t2_status_100826/`); the coworker who decides on T = 2 answered with two tries on new development seeds, after which the T = 2 protocol is settled and T = 3 starts (Appendix A of the prompt, verbatim; the coworker's document `Multistage1011261.docx` was **not provided**, so its SHA-256 could not be checked, `00_housekeeping.md` section 1). Solver v2.0 (`protocols/v2_T2_locked_v2_0.json`) stays the locked baseline. Seven arms, two experiments, 280 runs on the seeds 10511-10530 (20 per q, paired across arms):

- **E1, the stage-2 tie bias.** Does an actor with LeakyReLU hidden units and a smooth, unclamped mean map (`lrsm`), on top of MS-R3's `relu_st_s16` recipe (stratified starts, the s = 16 noise landing, 2800 stage-2 updates), bring the mean |peak error| to <= 0.04 at both q with a paired improvement over `t1_st_s16` in the 95 % interval and no G-A, G-F, G-N or G-S failure? `t1_st_s16` and `relu_st_s16` are re-run as controls on the same seeds. Only the combined change is reported.
- **E2, does the development DP-BR help PPO training.** With the t1 actor, the settings and the budgets of v2.0 fixed (stage 2 1600 updates, stage 1 600) and no stop rule and no polishing, does the sampling feedback (fixed stratified sampling alpha = 0; residual-guided sampling alpha = 0.5; residual + deviation-gain sampling alpha = 0.5) lower the area under the stage-2 eta_2/DW learning curve by at least 20 % at fixed checkpoints, with a paired interval that supports it and without a worse final gate set or stage-1 deviation gain? The sampling weights are recorded after every check; the closed form is used for offline evaluation only.

Out of scope (D1): no lock, no confirmation, no combined E1 + E2 arm, no T = 3 training run (reduced T = 3 smoke tests only), no protocol change, no fresh seeds (30501-30520 and 40501-40520 appear in no config of this round; `tools/ms/r4_launch.py` refuses them).

## 3. The E1 candidate actor `lrsm` (D3), as implemented

- **Forward** (`agents/ppo_curriculum.py`, `BetaActor.forward` / `_hidden_variant`): LeakyReLU with negative slope 0.01 (`F.leaky_relu(x, 0.01)`) in both hidden layers, the d component of the input not scaled; mean `mu = m + (1 - 2 m) sigmoid(z0)` with `m = mu_clamp = 1e-6` in float32, **no clamp anywhere on the mean of this variant** (the clamp stays in the path of `t1`, `relu` and `t10`, whose forwards are bitwise unchanged: `tests/test_ms_r4_actor.py::test_the_forwards_of_t1_relu_and_t10_are_unchanged_bit_for_bit` against the base commit's module); concentration `c = c_min + softplus(z1)` times `conc_scale`, the critic and the clipping of sampled actions to [1e-6, 1 - 1e-6] unchanged. In float32 the derivative with respect to z0 is positive for about -88 < z0 < 17 (tested at the saturation points) against -13.8 < z0 < 13.8 under the clamp; at z0 = -20 and +15 the clamp variants have a zero gradient and `lrsm` a positive one (tested).
- **Registration:** `ACTOR_VARIANTS` stays `("t1", "relu", "t10")` (MS-R3's tests iterate it and compare it with `tools/ms/r3_analysis.R3.ACTORS`; tested unchanged); `ACTOR_VARIANTS_R4 = ("lrsm",)` and `ALL_ACTOR_VARIANTS` (the union) are accepted by `CurriculumPPO.set_actor_variant`, `mean_effort_numpy` (the float32 numpy reload, variant-aware; `variant=None` reads the export's `actor_variant` entry), `tools/ms/replay_dev_rule.build_actor` and the runner (`actor_variant` "lrsm" besides "relu" and "t10"; "t1" is still refused so that a t1 run writes no variant field).
- **Initial weights (C-INIT):** the variant is set after construction, exactly as for `relu` / `t10`, so the initial actor and critic weights come from the same generator calls in the same order (test: identical across `t1`, `relu`, `lrsm` for a (q, seed); the runner records `init_state_sha256` in every manifest and the launch check compares them across the three E1 arms of every (q, seed)).
- **Records and carriers:** `actor_variant` = "lrsm" is in `run_config.json`, `manifest.json`, every weight export (`actor_variant` string entry) and the full states; the opponent, the refresh and the frozen snapshot carry it (tests).
- **No silent reload:** every loader used in this round either builds the `lrsm` forward from the export (`replay_dev_rule.build_actor`, behind `tools/ms/r4_curves.py`; `mean_effort_numpy`) or refuses it (`tools/ms/r3_actor_diagnostics.export_variant`, `tools/ms/residual_asymmetry_example.py`: tested to raise). No `tools/v2` policy / actor loader is used on MS-R4 exports (a test parses the imports of the new tools; the array and CSV comparison helpers of `tools/v2/cr1_compare` reach the launch checks only through the unchanged `tools/ms/launch_checks.py`). The stage-1 continuation table built from an `lrsm` snapshot agrees with direct evaluation of that snapshot's mean (test).

## 4. The E2 sampler (D4), as implemented

New optional runner keys; absent = MS-R3 behaviour bit for bit (tested against a checkout of `be2881ff`). Code: `utils/ms_focus.py` (new: `within_stratum_focus`, `within_strata_probs`), `utils/ms_rule.py` (`StageController` constructor arguments `focus_scope`, `focus_map`, `strata`; the g_bar EMA and the choice of focus; **not** `StageRule` fields, so the logged rule parameters of MS-R1..R3 are unchanged), `utils/ms_residual.py` (`StageDiag.gain_bins`, filled by `stage_diag(..., with_gain=True)` from the same verifier result), `run/run_ms_stagewise.py` (keys, validation, records). `envs/` is not changed; the draw stays `StartSampler.stratified_priority(t, n, rng, probs)`.

- **`focus_scope = "within_strata"`** (allowed only with `stratified_priority`, `fixed_global_sampler` true and the rule disabled; otherwise a config error). Notation: stage t >= 2, the strata of `StartSampler.strata(t, 20)` (tail T, near-tie P, middle M; counts n_T, n_P, n_M), lambda_T = n_T / n_bins, lambda_P = 0.35, lambda_M = 1 - lambda_P - lambda_T, alpha = `alpha_global`, `p(b) = lambda_T 1[b in T] / n_T + sum over S in {P, M} of lambda_S [(1 - alpha) 1[b in S] / n_S + alpha f_S(b)]`, `f_S` a distribution on S (uniform if the focus mass on S is 0 or not finite); no tail term and lambda_T = 0 for a stage without tail bins. **The three stratum masses are exactly lambda_T, lambda_P, lambda_M at every update** (to 1e-12; tested for any focus and alpha, and checked on every real run by the launch checks). **Before the first valid check (no map yet) p is `StartSampler.stratified_bin_probs(t, 0.35, 20.0, alpha=0.0)`, called**, so every E2 arm is bit-identical through local update 25 (C-FB25). `within_strata_probs` does not renormalise its focus: the exact stratum masses hold for a focus built by `within_stratum_focus` (the runner's only path), and the launch check "strata masses" tests every recorded probability vector (review finding R4-14).
- **Weight for `fb_r`:** the existing residual map rho_bar, the EMA of rho_b = max over the development nodes d of non-tail bin b of r_t(d) / s_t (`utils/ms_residual.py`), NaN read as 0.
- **`focus_map = "residual_gain"`** (requires `focus_scope`): from the **same** development verifier call, no extra call (tested: `fb_r` and `fb_rg` call `evaluate` the same number of times with the same arguments), g_b = max over the same nodes of Delta_t(d) / DW with Delta_t = `res.stages[t].delta` (non-negative by construction); g_bar the EMA of g_b with the same weight 0.5 initialised at the first valid check; within each stratum S: `f_S(b) = 1/2 rho_bar(b) / sum_S rho_bar + 1/2 g_bar(b) / sum_S g_bar`; fallbacks: g_bar sum over S 0 -> the normalised rho_bar term alone; rho_bar sum 0 -> the g_bar term alone; both 0 -> uniform on S (tested per stratum, with NaN and non-finite entries).
- **No closed form.** Nothing in the sampler, the focus, the controller or the schedule reads the closed form; `tests/test_ms_r4_focus.py::test_sampler_decisions_do_not_depend_on_the_closed_form_with_the_new_keys` repeats `tests/test_ms_runner.py::test_decisions_and_sampler_do_not_depend_on_the_closed_form` for `fb_r` and `fb_rg` (the closed-form functions replaced by stubs change neither the checks' D3 columns, nor the probabilities, nor the focus and gain maps, nor the training state).
- **Records.** For an arm with a new key `ms_binmaps_stage{t}.npz` additionally holds, per check, `focus_within`, `probs_next` (the bin probabilities in force from the next update; NaN at the last check of the stage, whose `p_digest_next` is empty) and, with `focus_map`, `gain` and `gain_bar`; for an arm without the new keys every output array is unchanged (tested). T-generic: the reduced T = 3 smoke runs with both keys.
- **`fb_rx`** is the existing cross-stratum sampler (the sampler inside E1's arms) and equals `t1_st_s16` bit for bit through local update 1201 (check C-X2).

## 6. The PI's adjustments and their reasons (binding; the reports must name each)

1. **Strata shares in E2.** The existing sampler fixes only the tail share; at alpha 0.5 the near-tie and middle shares move with the focus (MS-R1 `MS_s35a5`: 0.234 / 0.266 at q = 50, 0.259 / 0.286 at q = 60 against the design 0.35 / 0.15 and 0.35 / 0.195; MS-R3 `t1_st_s16`: 0.232 / 0.268 and 0.236 / 0.309; reproduced in `01_preamble_numbers.md` section 1). Arm (2) minus arm (1) would mix the feedback with a change of the strata shares, against the coworker's "same strata shares". Arms (2) and (3) therefore use the within-stratum focus (section 4); `fb_rx`, the existing sampler, is kept as a fourth, descriptive arm. The price is a weaker lever than the cross-stratum sampler.
2. **E1's arms already contain DP-BR feedback.** MS-R3's `*_st_*` arms use `fixed_global_sampler` with `alpha_global` 0.5, i.e. the sampler of `fb_rx`. E2's outcome governs the *claim* about DP-BR feedback; it does not remove the feedback from E1's configuration, because that would be an untested configuration. The reports say so.
3. **The learning curve is evaluated on the final tier, offline.** Arm (3) samples by the development-grid deviation gains and the development-tier eta_2 is the maximum of those same gains; scoring E2 with it would score (3) by its own target. The primary curve is the final-tier eta_2/DW evaluated offline on the weight exports; the development-tier online values are secondary.
4. **Operational definitions** of the coworker's targets are D6 (section 8 here).
5. **Prior information for E2** (PI-side, reproduced in `01_preamble_numbers.md` section 2, every number matches): the first 200 updates hold 49-75 % of the trapezoidal area of the development-tier eta_2/DW in the MS-R1 pilot; the cross-stratum alpha 0.5 sampler did not lower the full-window area (reduction -0.067 ... +0.067 at q = 50, -0.192 ... -0.146 at q = 60). The coworker's area stays the primary statistic; the secondary statistics are pre-registered so that a miss can be read.
6. **Power of E1's zero-failure target.** Zero failures in 40 runs bound the per-run failure rate by 8.8 % (exact Clopper-Pearson 95 % upper limit, reproduced: 0.0881); a candidate with `relu_st_s16`'s observed q = 50 rate (1 of 10) would pass all 20 q = 50 runs with probability 0.9^20 = 0.1216. Passing E1 screens out relu-sized failure rates; it does not establish robustness. The reports say so.
7. **The cost of the checks** (reproduced: median `verifier_sec` 0.0165 s over 11,520 stage-2 checks, median `update_wall_sec` 0.1608 s over 288,000 stage-2 updates, one check per 25 updates = 0.41 % of the stage-2 wall time). Prediction E2-P3 (section 9) is about this.

## 8. Criteria and decision rules (D6)

The numeric parameters are in `reports/ms/r4/prereg_criteria.json` (SHA-256 `9b8b1a5558618aa297cad8bf99cb20d0e1df4602d6cba5a162248abdf3632393`, committed with this file, before the launch): 0.04, 0.20, 0.001, the checkpoint grids (25 ... 1600 step 25, 64 points, divisor 1575), the late window (200-1600, divisor 1400), the level 0.005, the bootstrap size 10,000 and the seeds 20261013 (E1) and 20261014 (E2), the intervals (95 %: percentiles 2.5 / 97.5; 97.5 %: 1.25 / 98.75). The analysis tools read them from there. **Bootstrap:** 10,000 resamples of the 20 seeds of a q with replacement, indices `rng.integers(0, n, size=(10000, n))` of a fresh `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)` per (q, statistic), percentile intervals; pairing by (q, seed). The gates are the runner's own verdicts in `gates.json` (thresholds of `protocols/v2_T2_locked_v2_0.json`): G-A, G-F, G-N and G-S.

**E1 target** (all three parts at both q):
- (E1-a) the mean |peak error| (`stage2_peak_rel_err_abs`, final tier, of the frozen terminal stage) of `lrsm_st_s16` over its 20 runs <= 0.04;
- (E1-b) the 95 % interval of the mean paired difference of |peak error|, `lrsm_st_s16` - `t1_st_s16`, lies entirely below 0;
- (E1-c) none of the 40 `lrsm_st_s16` runs fails G-A, G-F, G-N or G-S, and none exits nonzero.
- **Met** iff (a), (b) and (c) hold at both q; **not met**: the line stops, no other slope, map or variant follows; **attribution:** met or not, the effect is that of the combined change, not of the LeakyReLU or of the mean map.
- Implementation note (a clarification of "20 runs"): a part that needs all 20 runs (or all 20 pairs) is reported `False` with the status `incomplete` when a planned run is missing or failed; a missing run counts as a failed run in (c). Inclusive bounds (`<=`).
- Descriptive: the same paired statistics for `lrsm - relu` and `relu - t1`; gate failures per arm and q with exact Clopper-Pearson 95 % intervals; the paired changes of the gap, the smoothing part, the remainder, sigma_2(0), RMSE_pos, tail mean, eta_2, R0, w_eff, the stage-1 error and Gmax_full/DW.

**E2 target**, for X in {`fb_r`, `fb_rg`} against `fb_a0`:
- **Area per run:** A = (1 / 1575) x the trapezoidal area of the final-tier eta_2/DW over local updates 25 ... 1600 (the 64 exports); **reduction per q:** rho_X = 1 - mean(A_X) / mean(A_fb_a0) over the 20 seeds, its interval from the paired bootstrap (the seeds resampled jointly for X and `fb_a0`, the ratio of means recomputed);
- (E2-a) rho_X >= 0.20 and the lower end of its 95 % interval > 0, at both q;
- (E2-b) no (q, seed) that passes all four gates under `fb_a0` fails any gate under X (a missing or failed X run fails);
- (E2-c) Gmax_full/DW at the end of stage 1 (final tier): the upper end of the 95 % interval of the mean paired difference X - `fb_a0` is <= +0.001 at both q (the G-N tolerance as the non-inferiority margin; for scale, MS-R3's `t1_st_s1` had medians of 0.00105 at q = 50 and 0.00048 at q = 60);
- **X meets the target** iff (a), (b) and (c) hold. **Order of the decision:** `fb_r` first; if it meets the target the statement "development-DP-BR sampling feedback lowers the area under the stage-2 eta_2/DW curve at this budget" rests on `fb_r` and `fb_rg` is reported descriptively; otherwise `fb_rg` is evaluated and, if it meets the target, the statement rests on it; if neither meets it the development DP-BR is kept as a verifier only and no training improvement is claimed. 97.5 % intervals are reported next to the 95 % ones (descriptive). `fb_rx` against `fb_a0`: the same statistics, descriptive, outside the decision. A part that needs all 20 pairs is `False` with `incomplete` when a pair is missing.
- **Secondary statistics (descriptive):** the mean of log10(eta_2/DW) over the 64 points; the area over local 200-1600 normalised by 1400; the first export with eta_2/DW <= 0.005; eta_2/DW at u1600; the area of the development-tier online values on the same 64 updates; |peak error|, gap, RMSE_pos and tail mean at the freeze; the stage-1 error; the realised strata shares; the cost. Paired against `fb_a0` with the 95 % interval (and rho for the area kinds).
- **Independent recomputation:** `tools/ms/r4_blind_e1.py` and `r4_blind_e2.py` recompute the criterion tables from the per-run tables and the curve files with their own code (they import no analysis module; a test parses their imports) and must agree with the analysis tables to 1e-12; the area is re-derived from the curve file.
