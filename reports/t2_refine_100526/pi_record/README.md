# T=2 refinement rounds (R1, v2.0, R2b, R2c): PI-side record

This folder is the PI-side record of the four rounds summarised in `reports/t2_refine_100526/100526report.md`: one row per prompt, with the PI's decisions at the gate that followed it and the round's outcome. It follows the model of `reports/v2/pi_record/00_README.md`. `prompts/` is meant to hold the prompts to the implementation agent (here: the two transcriptions named below); `plans/` is meant to hold the PI's note of 2026-10-02 (`Multistage100226.docx`), which is not available (see below and `plans/README.md`). The reports call the PI "the owner"; both words mean the same person. Nothing is moved, renamed or edited: the round reports stay where they are and are cited by path, and numbers are quoted from the reports.

**Inputs that were not available.** The note `Multistage100226.docx` and the delivered prompts 12, 13, 14 and 15 were not in the PI inbox when this publication was built (the inbox directory did not exist), and no copy of the note or of prompts 12-14 exists in any worktree or results folder. The publication prompt (decision D4) says: use a copy saved in the round's worktree or results and state the source; if none exists, leave the row and say so. Therefore:
- Prompts 12, 13, 14: rows left, file `not available`. Their "what it asked" and decision entries are read from the round reports, with path and section, and say nothing about the prompt text.
- Prompt 15 (R2c): the only copy is a transcription made by the assistant during the R2c session, of the text as it was pasted into that session (a provenance banner replaces one scratch line; line wrapping and emphasis may differ from the delivered file); it is included as `prompts/15_r2c_sampler_pilot_v2_1_prompt.md`, labelled a transcription, not the PI's delivered file.
- Prompt 16 (this publication): included as a transcription at `prompts/16_t2_refine_publication_prompt.md`.
- The note: no copy; `plans/` has no new file. The earlier plans are in `reports/v2/pi_record/plans/`.
