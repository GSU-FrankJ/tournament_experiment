## 6. D6: what is measured

The evaluated policy is always the last iterate, as Beta means: `bw` the composite of the frozen stage-3 snapshot, the frozen stage-2 snapshot and the live actor at stage 1 (`state_end_stage1.pt`, keys `frozen_stages` and `agent`); `jt`, `jt_es`, `jt2` the live actor at every stage (`state_end.pt`); `v20` the `u01600` export at t = 2 and the `u02200` export at t = 1. Exports are loaded with `tools/ms/replay_dev_rule.py` (`load_export`, `build_actor`, `candidate_fns`).

`tools/t3/t3_eval.py` computes per run, on the final and the development tier (`utils.v2_metrics.evaluate`, pure, plus `tools/t3/t3_metrics.py`), every payoff quantity divided by dW:

1. `Gmax_full_over_dw` (with `Gmax_full_t`, `Gmax_full_d`). 2. Per stage t = 1 ... T: `G_max_t{t}_over_dw`, `Delta_max_t{t}_over_dw` (eta_t) with their argmax gaps. 3. `EXP_root_over_dw`, `dReach_over_dw`, `Deltamax_all_over_dw` (and `dFull_over_dw`, `eta_T_over_dw`). 4. The band split at every t >= 2: the band is {d on the stage-t grid: |d| < 2q(t-1)}, a node at exactly |d| = 2q(t-1) is out of band (implemented as |d| < 2q(t-1) - 1e-9, so a node on the edge up to float error is out of band; on the verifier grids the edge is an exact node); `Delta_in_t{t}`, `Delta_out_t{t}`, `G_in_t{t}`, `G_out_t{t}`, with argmax gaps and node counts. 5. The terminal-stage recovery against the closed-form tent `g2_two_stage`, for any T, on the symmetric grid of D_T with step 0.5: `rmse_pos_T_over_eT0` (|d| < 2q), `tail_mean_T_over_eT0` and `tail_max_T_over_eT0` (|d| >= 2q), `peak_T_signed`, `peak_T_abs`, `eT_at_0`. At T = 2 these equal `evaluate`'s `stage2_rmse_pos_over_g2_0`, `stage2_tail_mean_over_g2_0`, `stage2_tail_max_over_g2_0` and `stage2_peak_rel_err_signed` bit for bit (tested). **Tail-set note:** at T = 3 the tail set |d| >= 2q within D_3 is 100 to 400 per side at q = 50 (440 at q = 60), against 100 to 200 per side at T = 2; the threshold 0.02 of G-A is v2.0's but the set is larger. 6. `e1_at_0`; at T = 2 also `stage1_rel_err_signed` and `stage1_rel_err_abs` against g1 = dW/(6kq). 7. The maximum normalised Beta standard deviation per stage on the tier grid (`conc_max_std_norm_t{t}`). 8. Gates with v2.0's thresholds, every "<=" inclusive at full float64 precision: G-A (eta_T <= 0.005, `rmse_pos_T_over_eT0` <= 0.05, `tail_mean_T_over_eT0` <= 0.02, final tier), G-F (`Gmax_full_over_dw` <= 0.01, final), G-N (|eta_T(dev) - eta_T(final)| <= 0.001 and |Gmax_full(dev) - Gmax_full(final)| <= 0.001), G-S (T = 2 only, `stage1_rel_err_abs` <= 0.05); run pass = G-A and G-F and G-N (and G-S at T = 2) and a clean exit and a passing global-RNG assertion. For `bw` the gate values come from the final composite, which equals v2.0's timing because eta_T and the terminal recovery depend only on the frozen stage-T policy. 9. Best of run (`jt`, `jt_es`, `jt2`; descriptive; "selected with the development verifier; not a pre-specified candidate"): the candidates are the valid development checks plus the last iterate (its development value is that of `final_eval_development.npz`, taken from `joint_run_summary.json`); a check whose evaluation is invalid or raised an error is excluded; the smallest development `Gmax_full_over_dw` wins, ties go to the earliest update; the winner is evaluated on both tiers from its export if it is a check (`check_every` = 25 = `weights_every`, so every check has an export) and from `state_end.pt` if it is the last iterate (`jt` and `jt_es` end at updates 1,267 and 1,754, which are not multiples of 25 and have no export). 10. Cost: updates, policy rows per stage, minibatch steps, wall and CPU time per phase and category (`bw`: `ms_run_summary.json` for the updates, the wall time per phase and category and the CPU time per phase, `gates.json` (`budget_totals`) for the minibatch steps, `status.json`; joint: `joint_run_summary.json`; `v20`: `v2_run_summary.json`, `v2_updates.csv`). The MS runner does not record a peak RSS. 11. Coverage: for the joint arms the visitation per stage and bin by start stage and the share of each stage's rows in band; for `bw` the expected direct starts per bin and the realised `n_start_tail`, `n_start_near`, `n_start_mid` of `ms_updates.csv`.

Curves (descriptive, against cumulative policy rows): the joint arms' development checks every 25 updates (`Gmax_full`, EXP_root, eta_t and G_max_t per stage) and `bw`'s per-phase checks (`ms_checks_stage{t}.csv`, the stage's own `Delta`).

## 7. D7: criteria and decision rules

`reports/t3/r1/prereg_criteria.json` (SHA-256 `b586a5e39b1fad503282b1bb903696ad7d47c52e75fb9ff6e47f06781ad88332`) holds the gate thresholds and the band definition, the hypotheses and their pairs, the bootstrap definition and the reading rule; `tools/t3/t3_analysis.py` and `tools/t3/t3_blind.py` read it. Pairing by (q, seed); n = the number of complete pairs of that (q, hypothesis); bootstrap indices `numpy.random.default_rng(S).integers(0, n, size=(10000, n))` created afresh with the same S for every (hypothesis, q, statistic), S = 20261016 at T = 3 and 20261017 at T = 2; percentile intervals of the 10,000 resampled means (`numpy.percentile`, linear): 95 % (2.5, 97.5), 98.75 % (0.625, 99.375; Bonferroni over four hypotheses; descriptive); the median's 95 % interval from the same indices; the analysis imports `paired_stats`, `boot_mean_ci`, `boot_indices` and `clopper_pearson` from `tools/ms/r4_common.py` (whose definition is this one), the blind tool reimplements everything. A run with a nonzero exit counts as failed in the gate counts, its metrics are missing and its pair is dropped; the number of dropped pairs is reported.

| hypothesis | horizon | paired difference on `Gmax_full_over_dw` (final tier) |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | T = 3 | `bw` - `jt` |
| H2 | T = 3 | `bw` - `jt_es` |
| H3 | T = 3 | `jt_es` - `jt` |
| H1-T2 | T = 2 | `v20` - `jt2` |

What the hypotheses read (the `reads` fields of the criteria file): H1, the backward order against the original joint scheme; H2, whether `bw` still has the lower full-domain gain once the joint scheme has exploring starts (the two schemes as implemented, not a coverage-matched test of the backward order); H3, whether the coverage that exploring starts give helps the joint scheme; H1-T2, H1 at T = 2 on the v2.0 confirmation seeds 30501-30520 (re-evaluated recorded runs against the new arm).

A hypothesis is **supported** iff the upper end of the 95 % interval of the mean paired difference is strictly below 0 at q = 50 and at q = 60; otherwise it is not supported and the report names the q at which it fails. Also reported: the 98.75 % interval, the median paired difference with its interval, the number of seeds with a negative difference, the secondary statistics for the four pairs (`EXP_root_over_dw`, eta_t and G_max_t per t, the band split at t >= 2, `dReach_over_dw`, `rmse_pos_T_over_eT0`, `tail_mean_T_over_eT0`, `peak_T_abs`, `e1_at_0`, at T = 2 the stage-1 error), and the gate pass counts per arm and q with exact Clopper-Pearson 95 % intervals. **Reading of `bw` at T = 3** (this round decides nothing): if `bw` passes G-A and G-F and G-N in at least 18 of 20 runs at both q, v2.0's design at T = 3 is a candidate for a T = 3 lock (the next round would lock it and confirm it on a fresh T = 3 seed block); otherwise the reports give the per-gate, per-stage diagnosis and no change is tried in this round.

## 8. D8: predictions (the PI's, written before the launch; expectations, not tests; nothing is selected by them)
