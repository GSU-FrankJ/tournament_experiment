# R1 decision inputs: the six methods side by side

Generated by `tools/v2/refine_analysis.py decision` from the wave tables written by the `stage1` and `stage2` sub-commands (`results/v2_refine/analysis/stage1_*.csv`, `stage2_*.csv`); no number is typed by hand and none is recomputed here. All differences are arm - baseline paired by (q, seed) (stage 1: arm - `B_base`; stage 2: arm - `A_base`); the primary metric is `|e1_hat(0) - e1*|/e1*` for stage-1 arms and the absolute signed peak error for stage-2 arms (improvement = negative difference); the confidence intervals are the pre-registered 95% percentile bootstrap intervals (10000 resamples, `default_rng(20261003)`). Criterion part (a): the CI of the mean difference is below 0 at both q; part (b) (the gate part): no run that passed its gate under the baseline fails it under the arm. The criterion is descriptive, not a gate. Dispersion ratio = across-seed SD of the signed error of the arm over the baseline's with a paired-resampling CI.

## Observations (what the data show; not recommendations)

Statements below restate numbers of this report and of the cited tables; none is an inference beyond what the tables show, and none is a recommendation. Hypotheses that the data do not distinguish are labelled as such. Differences are arm - baseline, paired by (q, seed), n = 10 per q; intervals are the pre-registered 95% percentile bootstrap intervals.

**Run hygiene and checks.**
- 20 of 20 C-R1 runs, 20 of 20 `parents_A` end-of-A states, 20 of 20 `B_base` runs and 20 of 20 `A_base` runs reproduce `rehearsal_v1_1` in the training-relevant state (`results/v2_refine/v11_reproduction_checks.json`, `parents_A_checks.json`, `stage1_base_checks.json`, `stage2_base_checks.json`). All 160 stage-1 runs have a frozen stage-2 snapshot bit-identical to the parent's actor (`results/v2_refine/analysis/stage1_decomposition.csv`, column `n_frozen_bit_identical`), so the induced target e~1 and its band are shared by every arm of a (q, seed).
- All 160 stage-1 runs pass G-F and G-N, and all 220 stage-2 runs pass G-A and G-N (`results/v2_refine/analysis/stage1_gate_counts.csv`, `stage2_gate_counts.csv`): the criterion part (b) (no baseline-passing run fails under the arm) therefore holds for every arm trivially, and the gates do not discriminate between arms in this wave.

**Stage 1 (the S1 error |e^_1(0) - e_1*| / e_1*, `results/v2_refine/analysis/stage1_overview.csv`, `stage1_criterion.csv`).**
- `B_expcont` is the only stage-1 arm whose confidence interval of the mean paired difference lies below 0 at both q: -0.02386 [-0.04061, -0.009831] at q = 50 and -0.03783 [-0.06321, -0.01472] at q = 60, with 9 of 10 seeds better at each q. The medians of the S1 error are 0.0354 (q = 50) and 0.0451 (q = 60) for `B_base` and 0.0120 and 0.0101 for `B_expcont`. The number of runs within the 0.05 target of the 0929 discussion is 8 and 6 of 10 for `B_base` and 10 and 10 of 10 for `B_expcont` (`stage1_gate_counts.csv`, `n_target0929_pass`).
- The signed error does not move: `B_expcont` - `B_base` = +0.001725 [-0.0217, 0.02755] (q = 50) and +0.003013 [-0.03678, 0.04258] (q = 60). The improvement of the S1 error is a reduction of the spread: the across-seed SD of the signed error is 0.01785 against 0.05153 (ratio 0.3464 [0.2006, 0.6171]) at q = 50 and 0.01874 against 0.06976 (ratio 0.2687 [0.1532, 0.5032]) at q = 60 (`stage1_dispersion.csv`), and the within-run SD of e^_1(0) over the last five weight exports falls by -0.7027 [-1.036, -0.3923] and -0.4303 [-0.7239, -0.1842] effort units (`stage1_paired.csv`).
- The SD of the raw stage-1 advantages is 0.0656 (q = 50) and 0.0531 (q = 60) times the baseline's (mean 0.0795 and 0.0630 against 1.211 and 1.186; `stage1_adv_ratio.csv`). The phase wall time is 1.084 times the baseline's, including the table build of 7 to 8 s per run (`stage1_cost.csv`, `results/v2_refine/continuation_check.json`).
- The other six stage-1 arms (`B_polish1`, `B_polish2`, `B_batch`, `B_batch_mb256`, `B_kl005`, `B_kl010`): the interval of the mean paired difference of the S1 error contains 0 at both q for each of them. `B_batch_mb256` has 8 of 10 seeds better at q = 60 (mean -0.01981 [-0.04952, 0.008318]). Wall time relative to `B_base`: `B_batch` 1.362, `B_batch_mb256` 3.683, `B_kl005` 0.3337, `B_kl010` 0.5924 (`stage1_cost.csv`). For the target-KL arms the share of updates with fewer than 10 epochs run is 0.9408 / 0.942 (`B_kl005`, q = 50 / 60) and 0.6648 / 0.6602 (`B_kl010`) (`stage1_stop_epoch.csv`).
- Check (ii) of the continuation table (the basis of `B_expcont`) is open as stated: the literal final-tier criterion (<= 1e-6 DW) is not met (3.229e-05 DW at q = 50, 1.960e-05 DW at q = 60); on the refined verifier configuration the gap is 4.982e-07 / 4.100e-07 DW (section 3 above, `results/v2_refine/continuation_check.json`). The PI decided to keep method 6 in the round.
