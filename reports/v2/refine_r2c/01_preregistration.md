## 3. Criterion, selection rule, bootstrap (D3)

**Criterion per arm** (as in R1 / R2b), against `parents_A`, paired by (q, seed):
(a) the 95% percentile bootstrap CI of the mean paired difference (arm - baseline) of the absolute signed peak error excludes 0 in the improving direction (negative difference) at **both** q;
(b) no run that passed G-A and its G-N part under the baseline fails it under the arm.
Bootstrap: 10,000 resamples of the 10 paired seeds with replacement, `numpy.random.default_rng(20261005)`, one fresh generator per (q, statistic) in table order; the CI is the 2.5 / 97.5 percentiles of the resampled mean. The primary metric `stage2_peak_rel_err_abs` = |(e2-hat(0) - e2*(0)) / e2*(0)| of the stage-2 last iterate at the end of Phase A (global update 1600); the closed form enters the evaluation only. Definitions of the verdicts as in v2.0: G-A = eta_2 / DW <= 0.005 (final verifier tier) and RMSE_pos / e2*(0) <= 0.05 and tail mean / e2*(0) <= 0.02; the G-N part = |dev - final| <= 0.001 for eta_2 / DW (development tier minus final tier). The primary metric, RMSE_pos and the tail mean are recovery-grid metrics (symmetric grid on D_2, step 0.5, `utils.v2_metrics.recovery_metrics`) and do not depend on the verifier tier; RMSE_pos is the root mean square of e2-hat - e2* over the grid nodes with |d| < 2q divided by e2*(0), and the tail mean is the mean of e2-hat(d) over the grid nodes with |d| >= 2q divided by e2*(0) (`protocols/v2_T2_locked_v2_0.json`, gates G-A).

**Selection rule.** Among the arms meeting (a) and (b): the largest total number of runs with |peak error| <= 0.05 over both q; tie -> the smaller mean |peak error| over both q; further tie -> the smaller change from the locked sampler (later `local_first`, then smaller share). The selected arm's `start_weights` is the one pipeline change of v2.1. If no arm meets (a) and (b): write the pilot reports and stop (§6); the rule is not relaxed and no arm is added.

**Choices that make the rule mechanical** (the prompt leaves them open; they are implemented in `select_arm` of `tools/v2/refine_r2c_analysis.py` and stated again in `03_selection.md`):
1. "|peak error|" is the primary metric above; "runs with |peak error| <= 0.05" is the count, over the 20 runs of both q, with primary <= 0.05 (inclusive); "mean |peak error| over both q" is the mean of the primary over those 20 runs (n = 10 per q, so it equals the mean of the two per-q means).
2. An arm with a missing or incomplete run cannot be selected; the missing run is reported, never skipped.
3. The third tie-break compares (-`local_first`, `peak_share`) ascending: the arm with the later `local_first` wins, then the one with the smaller share. Ties of the second criterion are exact float ties (they are not expected to occur).
4. Criterion part (a) at a q requires the CI upper bound of the mean difference to be < 0; part (b) is evaluated over all runs, both q, exactly as the R2b tool does (`b_status`, `b_violations`).
5. The reference rows of R2b's `A_peak25` and `A_peak50` (from update 1) are shown in the cross-arm tables for the share response only; they are never eligible and are not re-run.

The analysis writes `results/v2_refine_r2c/analysis/selection.json` (the rule, the inputs per arm, the ranking, the selected arm or null). Metrics of the stage-2 analysis (as in R2b): primary absolute signed peak error; location-free peak with its argmax d; RMSE_pos; tail mean and max with the G-A verdict; eta_2 with the G-A and G-N verdicts; symmetry error; sigma_2(0); smoothed-game share of the d = 0 gap; per arm and q the tail statistics (max |peak error|, number of runs with |peak error| <= 0.05, number with eta_2 / DW > 0.004); the peak-set visitation share; KL, clip fraction, wall time. Every run is reported, failures included.

## 5. Gate G-P (D5)

**G-P** (stage-2 peak recovery): |e2-hat(0) - e2*(0)| / e2*(0) <= 0.10 on the end-of-A last iterate (the stage-2 analogue of G-S). **Admission rule**, evaluated on the v2.1 re-rehearsal (§4.4 R3b) and recorded in `results/v2_T2_locked/v2_1/gp_rule.json` before the confirmation launch (the v2.1 analysis script reads it): G-P enters run pass iff (i) at least 19 of the 20 rehearsal runs satisfy it, and (ii) under a normal model fitted per q to the rehearsal's signed peak errors (mean, SD with ddof = 1; the other gates treated as passing) the probability that at least 18 of 20 fresh runs satisfy G-P is >= 0.90 at both q. If the rule fails, G-P is reported, not gated, and run pass stays G-A and G-F and G-N and G-S. Reported for every run either way: G-P, the peak error with its decomposition, and the v2.0 / v1.1 / v1.0 outcomes.

**Choice (how (ii) is computed):** per q, with x ~ N(mu_q, s_q^2) and mu_q, s_q the mean and the ddof = 1 SD of the 10 signed peak errors of the rehearsal at that q, the per-run pass probability is p_q = Phi((0.10 - mu_q) / s_q) - Phi((-0.10 - mu_q) / s_q); the number of passing runs among 20 fresh runs is Binomial(20, p_q) and (ii) requires P(>= 18) >= 0.90 for both q. Condition (i) counts the 20 rehearsal runs of both q together (>= 19 of 20). The model, p_q and the probabilities are written to `gp_rule.json`.

## 8. Implementation (what the code does)

**Sampler schedule.** `Run.draw_starts(stage, n, local)` draws bin-balanced when `start_weights` is absent / null / `bin_balanced`, or when `local < local_first` (default 1), and peak-focused otherwise; `local` is the phase-local update index (1, ..., cap) passed by the three call sites (phase A / C loop, phase P loop). `StartSampler` (`envs/curriculum_env.py`) is not edited. Validation: `peak_focused` accepts the optional key `local_first`, an int >= 1 (bool, float, string, null refused); `bin_balanced` still takes no other key; unknown keys are refused as before; the modes are those of R2b (`phase_A`, `phase_A_continue`, `phase_P`). The config value is written to `run_config.json` and to `manifest.json` (`start_weights`, including `local_first` when given). **Choice:** mode `locked` (the entry point of the lock rounds) does not accept `start_weights` in this code; the v2.1 entry point of §4.3 changes that, if an arm is selected, before the lock.

**Stream consumption.** Before `local_first` the calls are `balanced`'s (`rng.integers`, `rng.random`) and the roles draw follows, exactly as without `start_weights`; from `local_first` on the calls are `peak_focused`'s (two `rng.random(n)`); so the `start` stream is identical to the baseline's for the updates before `local_first` and diverges from then on. With `local_first` = 1 the arm is R2b's `A_peak` sampler (tested bit-identical against the R2b code commit `1ff99bd`).

**Launcher.** `python tools/v2/launch_refine.py --wave r2c_waveS --code-commit <sha>` (root `results/v2_refine_r2c`, `--workers` at most 40, dry run first); `--wave r2c_v20_repro` is C-R3. A wave refuses seeds outside 10501-10510 and a run directory that already holds a run.
