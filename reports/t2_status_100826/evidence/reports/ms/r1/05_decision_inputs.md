## 3. What each mechanism adds at matched budget (rule arms minus `MS_base2400`)

Descriptive; every interval below is from `paired_vs_MS_base2400.csv` and contains 0 unless a `*` is shown in `04_pilot.md`.

- **The rule (stop and polishing alone, `MS_rule`).** The stop did not fire in any of the 100 rule-arm terminal-stage runs (all capped at 2000 training updates + 400 landing), so the rule arms differ from the control in the sampler and in the polishing blocks only. `MS_rule` minus the control: |peak| +0.0028 [-0.0258, +0.0327] (q = 50), +0.0103 [-0.0121, +0.0306] (q = 60); RMSE_pos +0.0044 [-0.0001, +0.0090] (q = 50). Polishing blocks were used in 1 of 10 runs at q = 50 and 9 of 10 at q = 60 (26 blocks); the q = 60 point estimate is on the worse side with a wide interval.
- **The samplers (lambda_P 0.25 / 0.35, alpha_global 0 / 0.5).** |peak| at q = 50: -0.0135 (`MS_s25a0`), -0.0101 (`MS_s25a5`), -0.0152 (`MS_s35a0`), -0.0188 (`MS_s35a5`); at q = 60: +0.0009, -0.0014, -0.0077, -0.0014; every interval contains 0 (upper ends +0.0040, +0.0053, +0.0071, +0.0018 at q = 50; +0.0161, +0.0099, +0.0076, +0.0108 at q = 60). eta_2 shows no resolved difference from the control and RMSE_pos only one (`MS_s25a5` at q = 60: -0.0037 [-0.0071, -0.0006] *); the tail-mean difference is slightly positive in every sampler arm at q = 50 (+0.0005 to +0.0010; `MS_s25a5` [+0.00004, +0.00205] *), i.e. the sampler arms do not lower the tail error below the control's.
- **Against `MS_rule`** (the sampler's effect net of the rule and polishing): |peak| -0.0129 to -0.0216 at q = 50 (all intervals contain 0) and, at q = 60, -0.0181 [-0.0333, -0.0035] * (`MS_s35a0`) and -0.0117 [-0.0244, -0.0004] * (`MS_s35a5`); RMSE_pos lower for `MS_s25a0` (q = 50), `MS_s25a5` (both q) and `MS_s35a5` (q = 50), intervals excluding 0 (`04_pilot.md` section 4, `paired_vs_MS_rule.csv`). `MS_rule` itself is the worst arm of the wave against the control at q = 60, which enlarges these differences.
- **Stage 1.** The stage-1 rule stopped 99 of 100 runs (median local update 112-175 per cell) and the median totals per cell are 2912-2975 updates against 3000 for the control (per run 2875-3400, 23 of the 100 above 3000). Stage-1 |error| minus the control: `MS_rule` +0.0074 [+0.0023, +0.0131] * (q = 50) and -0.0072 [-0.0146, -0.0002] * (q = 60), the other arms' intervals contain 0 except `MS_s35a0` at q = 60 (-0.0082 [-0.0119, -0.0042] *). All 140 runs pass G-S, G-F and G-N(Gmax).

## 4. What changed and what remains

- **Lower (descriptive, not separated from the budget).** Against `parents_A` the share of runs with |peak error| <= 0.05 rises from 7 of 20 to 12 of 20 with the budget alone and to 14-16 of 20 with a sampler (10 of 20 for `MS_rule`); the pooled mean |peak| falls from 0.0595 to 0.0490 (control) and 0.0375-0.0432 (samplers). The near-tie stratum, which the samplers oversample, has a lower RMSE at q = 50 in the four sampler arms than in `parents_A`, the control and `MS_rule` (1.1-1.9 effort units against 2.0-2.8; `04_pilot.md` 5.4).
- **Remains.** (i) The learned peak is below the closed form in every arm at both q: the signed peak error has a 95 % interval below 0 in all 14 arm x q cells (means -0.034 to -0.066). (ii) The smoothed-game share of the d = 0 gap, which is 0.52-0.55 (median, `MS_base`) rises with lambda_P at q = 50: 0.71 (lambda_P 0.25), 0.76-0.94 (0.35), and is 0.58-0.63 at q = 60 in the sampler arms; the share is larger in the sampler arms at q = 50, but the quantity is ill-conditioned near a zero gap and no causal reading is drawn from it. (iii) The middle stratum is not improved by the samplers (RMSE on d < 0 at q = 50: 1.4-1.8 for the bin-balanced-distribution arms, 1.5-2.1 for the sampler arms; the arm with the smallest middle start share, `MS_s35a0`, has the largest, 2.13). (iv) The tail is at the closed form up to a +0.5 effort-unit offset in every arm, as for `parents_A`; no arm has a tail problem (largest tail mean 0.0125 against the limit 0.02). (v) One run in 140 fails G-A (eta_2/DW 0.005054 against 0.005: `MS_s35a0` q = 50 seed 10508).

## 5. The stop rule and the residual

- At rho_2 = 0.05 the terminal-stage stop fired in 0 of 100 runs (the calibration expected about 1 in 60). R_2 <= 0.05 held at 1 of 4000 training checks at q = 50 and 91 of 4000 at q = 60, with at most 2 consecutive eligible checks. R_2 at the freeze (after the landing, at the lowest learning rate) has median 0.057-0.121 and is <= 0.05 in 14 of 140 runs. The calibration put the rho at which the stop fires at v2.0's quality at about 0.06-0.08 (`01_calibration.md` Table 2c); the pilot used the single pre-registered rho, so the pre-registered 0.05 made the stop a budget here, as `02_preregistration.md` section 3 anticipated.
- R0 = r_2(0)/s_2 tracks the peak error in the way the calibration and the linearised factor predict (|peak|/R0 median 1.656-1.679 at q = 50, 1.460-1.468 at q = 60 in every arm; 136 of 139 runs within about 5 % of the factor; the three exceptions have |peak| < 0.004), so a first-order residual read at d = 0 is informative about the peak where R, the maximum over the non-tail region, is not (the cell medians of R at the freeze are 1.9-5.2 times those of R0).
- The localized branch is reached mainly at q = 60 (26-34 of 50 classifications are localized, 1-6 at q = 50), as expected; S_t contains near-tie bins in 39-50 of 50 classifications; at q = 50 |S_t| (mean 8.0-9.3) is above the localized limit of 5 so the rule stays global.
- Stage 1: in 22 of 50 q = 50 firing streaks of the rule arms (4-5 of 10 per arm) a check has R_1 = 0 exactly (1 of 49 at q = 60), the verifier property recorded in `01_calibration.md` section 5.

## 6. What the pilot does not settle

- Whether the stop would fire at the quality of the best arms at some rho: untested (the pilot used the single pre-registered rho).
- Everything at T = 3: no T = 3 experiment was run (the code is exercised in tests only).
