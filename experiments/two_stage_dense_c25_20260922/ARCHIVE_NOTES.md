# Supporting development archive

This is a compact publication copy. Reports, settings and all-seed tables retain the recorded research results, including failures. Source-machine path prefixes were made repository-relative. Original checkpoints, dense weight exports, training histories, process logs, duplicate HTML/DOCX reports and diagnostic replay bundles remain in the full research archive and are not included here.

The numerical rows in CSV/JSON are archived outcomes, not newly rerun results. References in the dated report to omitted raw files describe that full archive. Follow [the portable T2 guide](../../MultiStage/two_stage/README.md) for current commands and the precise scope of each cohort. Old machine-specific commands in the dated report are historical. New outputs belong under scratch/, separately from these archived tables.
