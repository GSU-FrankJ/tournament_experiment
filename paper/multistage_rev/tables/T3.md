## Sources

- **A** `results/t3_r4/confirmation/eval/per_run.csv` (80 rows, arm bwA, one row per run): the run counts and `sum` of column `gates_pass` (`gate_G_A` and `gate_G_F` and `gate_G_N`) per q; equal to `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/counts.csv` columns `runs`, `pass` and to `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/headline.json` `counts`.
- **B** `protocols/v2_T3_locked_v1_1.json` key `confirmation.rule` (`max_failures_per_q` = limit per q, `max_failures_total` = limit of 80), applied to the failing counts; equal to `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/headline.json` `verdict`.
- **C** `results/t3_r4/confirmation/eval/per_run.csv`, the column named in the first column, median / minimum / maximum over the 40 rows of the q (numpy; the median of 40 values is the mean of the two middle values; signed errors keep their sign); the medians equal the `median_*` columns of `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/counts.csv` exactly (no `median_peak_T_abs` column is stored).
- **D** `results/t3_r4/confirmation/eval/per_run.csv` column `Gmax_full_over_dw`: median and maximum over the 40 rows of the q (equal to `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/counts.csv` columns `median_Gmax_full_over_dw`, `worst_Gmax_full`, `worst_Gmax_full_run`); the seed is column `seed` of the row of the maximum.
- **E** `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/cost.csv` columns `fired`, `fired_share`; `fired` equals the number of rows of `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/fired.csv` (with the q), `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/headline.json` `trigger.fired` (both q) and the number of run directories (untracked root `t3-r4-lock-confirm/results/t3_r4/confirmation/q{q}/seed{seed}/bwA/`, read-only) holding a `retry.json`.
- **F** `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/cost.csv` columns `mean_updates`, `mean_policy_rows`, `ratio_updates_to_not_fired`, `ratio_policy_rows_to_not_fired`, `median_wall_sec` (median over the runs of `total_wall_sec` of each run's `status.json`, the whole run including a restarted attempt); the two reference values are the sums of `protocols/v2_T3_locked_v1_1.json` `settings.budgets` and 512 `settings.episodes_per_update` times that sum.
- **G** `results/t3_r4/confirmation/eval/per_run.csv` (row q = 60, seed 80502): the gate columns `gate_G_A`, `gate_G_F`, `gate_G_N`, the values and the development-tier columns `dev_*`; the limits are the keys of `protocols/v2_T3_locked_v1_1.json` `gates` (equal to `reports/t3/r1/prereg_criteria.json` `gates`); the failing gates are listed in both `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/failures.csv` (column `failing_gates`) and `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/headline.json` (key `failures`, field `failing_gates`) and agree; the failing quantities (column `failing_quantities`) and the stage (column `Gmax_full_stage`) are only in `results/t3_r4/confirmation/analysis/failures.csv`, where they equal the values of this table.
