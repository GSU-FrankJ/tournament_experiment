# T2. T = 2 recovery and confirmation

Frozen v2.0 policies at T = 2, final-tier verifier: the confirmation runs stored as arm `v20` (20 per q) in `results/t3_r1/eval/per_run.csv`. Every number below is a row of `T2.csv`; signed errors keep their sign. Notation: e_1(0) and e_2(d) are the learned mean efforts at stages 1 and 2, g1 and g_2(d) the closed-form equilibrium; the tie point is d = 0, the positive-effort region is |d| < 2q and the theoretical zero-effort tails are |d| >= 2q (stage 2).

## Sources

1. `results/t3_r1/eval/per_run.csv`: the 20 rows with `arm == 'v20'` and `q == 50` (seeds 30501-30520) and the 20 rows with `q == 60`, column as named. Pass count: number of `True` in `gates_pass`. Median, min, max: numpy over the 20 rows (median of the even count = mean of the two middle values).
2. `results/t3_r1/eval/per_run.csv`: the single row with `arm == 'v20'`, `q == 50`, `seed == 30510`, column as named; gate verdicts from the columns `gate_G_A`, `gate_G_F`, `gate_G_N`, `gate_G_S`, `gates_pass`, `ga_eta`, `ga_rmse`, `ga_tail`, `gn_eta`, `gn_gmax`, `gs_s1`.
3. Limits of the sub-conditions: `reports/t3/r1/prereg_criteria.json` key `gates` (see C).
4. G-N value: derived as abs(`dev_eta_T_over_dw` - `eta_T_over_dw`) and abs(`dev_Gmax_full_over_dw` - `Gmax_full_over_dw`) of the same row (development-tier and final-tier columns).
5. `reports/t3/r1/prereg_criteria.json` key `gates` (G-A, G-F, G-N, G-S); each threshold equals the one in `protocols/v2_T2_locked_v2_0.json` key `gates` (metric names there: `stage2_rmse_pos_over_g2_0` for `rmse_pos_T_over_eT0`, `stage2_tail_mean_over_g2_0` for `tail_mean_T_over_eT0`).
6. No limit: the quantity has no key in `gates` of `reports/t3/r1/prereg_criteria.json` or `protocols/v2_T2_locked_v2_0.json`; the tie-point error is listed under `reported_not_gated.stage2` and `peak_error` ('not gated') of `protocols/v2_T2_locked_v2_0.json`; the G-S limit applies to the absolute stage-1 error only.
